# Sample Stories & Proposed Projects

## Impact Stories

### Story 1: From the Brink to the Classroom (Education Focus)
When 8-year-old Brian lost his primary caregiver, his school attendance ground to a complete halt. Without tuition fees or basic supplies like exercise books and uniforms, he spent his days helping in local markets just to secure a daily meal. Through the intervention of Kwagala Children's Foundation, Brian was provided with full scholastic support, daily meals, and an assigned mentor. Today, Brian ranks among the top students in his class and dreams of becoming a civil engineer.

### Story 2: Restoring Health & Peace of Mind (Healthcare Focus)
Growing up in an under-resourced community, 5-year-old Sarah suffered from severe, recurring treatable illnesses that went unaddressed due to lack of medical access and funds. When Kwagala’s community health outreach identified her situation, she received immediate medical evaluation, treatment, and ongoing nutritional assistance. Now healthy and active, Sarah spends her afternoons playing safely and learning alongside her peers.

## Project Allotments & Dollar-Impact Breakdown
Clear, transparent unit-cost breakdowns build immediate donor trust by illustrating exactly how every dollar generates measurable, real-world impact.

| Tier | Project / Focus Area | What $ Contributes | Direct Operational Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$1** | Daily Nutrition Program | 1 Balanced School Meal | Provides a nutritious, warm meal (maize porridge/beans/rice) ensuring a child stays focused throughout the school day. |
| **$5** | Basic Scholastic Kit | 1 Term of Essential Supplies | Covers exercise books, pens, pencils, and basic stationery for one student. |
| **$15** | Child Health & Hygiene | 1 Month of Healthcare Support | Funds basic medical check-ups, preventative care, and hygiene kits (soap, towels, dental care). |
| **$30** | Monthly Child Sponsorship | Full Comprehensive Care | Covers school fees, learning materials, two daily meals, and mentorship for one child for a full month. |
| **$100** | Emergency Family Relief | Household Stability Pack | Provides emergency food rations, bedding, and clean water access for a family fostering a vulnerable child. |
